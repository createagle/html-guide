Ridge Loop

A 14 km circuit that climbs from the north car park through pine forest to Eagle Ridge, follows the crest for three kilometres and drops to the lake before returning along the shore.

Getting there

Take the B road north from the city for 50 minutes. The car park fills by 9:00 on summer weekends; the hourly bus from the station stops at the entrance.

The climb

The first 4 km gain 450 m on a wide forest track. Benches mark each 100 m of height. Above the tree line the path becomes rocky and the views open up to the south.

On the ridge

The crest is exposed: carry a wind layer even on warm days, and turn back if cloud comes down. Two marked descents let you shorten the walk to 9 km.

Down to the lake

A stepped path drops steeply to the water. The lake path is flat but can be muddy after rain; the café at the boathouse is open from April to October.
