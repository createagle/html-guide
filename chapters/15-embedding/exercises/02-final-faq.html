Trail questions

Do I need a permit?

No permit is needed for day walks. Overnight camping at the second saddle needs a free permit from the visitor centre, which you can collect on the morning you set off.

Is there water on the ridge?

There is a spring at the second saddle that runs all year. Treat or filter the water. There is no water on the crest itself, so carry at least two litres for the second day.

Can I bring a dog?

Dogs are welcome on a lead. Sheep graze the lower slopes from April to September.
