Open Web Day 2026

A free, one-day community conference about building for the web with HTML.

Saturday 14 November 2026, 9:30–17:30 · Old Tram Depot, Millbrook · and online

Register free Add to calendar

A day for people who build the web

Open Web Day brings designers, developers and content people together for talks and hands-on workshops about the layer everything else stands on: HTML. Bring a laptop for the afternoon, or just bring questions.

  • 4 talks, streamed with live captions
  • 3 hands-on workshops in the afternoon
  • 180 seats in person, unlimited online

Venue

Old Tram Depot
40 Canal Road
Millbrook MB1 4AB

From Central Station, walk north on Station Street and cross the bridge over the Mill Canal. The depot is on your right, about five minutes from the station.

The entrance on Canal Road is step-free. There is a lift to every floor, accessible toilets, a hearing loop in the main hall and a quiet room.

    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. Our sponsors cover the venue, lunch and the stream. Registering lets us plan seats and food.

    Can I watch online?

    All four talks and the lightning talks are streamed with live captions. Workshops are in person only.

    Will the talks be recorded?

    Yes. Recordings with edited captions go online within a week.

    Can I change or cancel my registration?

    Reply to your ticket email and we’ll update it. Please cancel if you can’t come, so someone else can have the seat.

    Code of conduct

    We want Open Web Day to be welcoming for everyone. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated, in person or online. If something happens, talk to a volunteer in a yellow T-shirt or email conduct@example.org.

    Open Web Day is organised by the Millbrook Web Collective, a group of volunteers.
