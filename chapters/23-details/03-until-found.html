Planting

Plant tulip bulbs in autumn, about three times as deep as the bulb is tall.

Choose a sunny spot with soil that drains well; bulbs rot in wet ground. Point the tip upwards and leave a hand’s width between bulbs.

Watering

Water once after planting, then leave them alone until spring.

In spring, water only in dry weeks. Cover the bed with wire mesh if squirrels dig up the bulbs.

After flowering

Cut off the faded flowers but keep the leaves.

The leaves feed the bulb for next year. Remove them only when they have turned completely yellow, usually six weeks later.
