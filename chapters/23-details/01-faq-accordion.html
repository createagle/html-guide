How long does delivery take?

Orders placed before 2 pm ship the same day. Most arrive in two to three working days.

Can I return an item?

Yes, within 30 days. Use the prepaid label in the parcel; refunds reach your card in five days.

Do you ship abroad?

We ship to the EU, the UK and Switzerland. Customs fees are included in the price at checkout.

How do I change my address?

Open “Orders”, choose the order and press “Edit delivery”. This works until the parcel is packed.
