Ridge path reopens

The northern ridge path reopens on Saturday 4 OctoberMonday 6 October after a summer of repairs.

The steepest section now has new steps and a handrail. Parking at the lower car park costs £4 £3 a day.

Dogs are not allowed on the upper section.
