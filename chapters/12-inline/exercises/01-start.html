What is cold brew?

Published 3 October 2026 · Updated last Monday · 4 min read

Cold brew is coffee steeped in cold water for 12 to 18 hours instead of being brewed with hot water.

The result has less acidity, which matters if you follow the advice of the BDA (British Dietetic Association) on stomach-friendly drinks.

Our two blends for cold brew are Ridge Roast (code 4001) and Lake Roast (code 4002).
