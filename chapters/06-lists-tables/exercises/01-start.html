Making cold brew

1. Grind 100 g of coffee coarsely.
2. Stir it into 1 litre of cold water.
3. Leave it in the fridge for 12 to 18 hours.
4. Strain twice through a paper filter.

Words you will meet

Bloom: the bubbling when hot water first hits fresh grounds.
Concentrate: brew strong enough to be diluted before drinking.
Grind size: how coarse or fine the particles are.
