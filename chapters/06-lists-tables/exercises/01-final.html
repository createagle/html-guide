Making cold brew

  1. Grind 100 g of coffee coarsely.
  2. Stir it into 1 litre of cold water.
  3. Leave it in the fridge for 12 to 18 hours.
  4. Strain twice through a paper filter.

Words you will meet

Bloom
The bubbling when hot water first hits fresh grounds.
Concentrate
Brew strong enough to be diluted before drinking.
Grind size
How coarse or fine the particles are.
